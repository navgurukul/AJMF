ABOUT US

The Story Behind
Anish Jadhav Memorial Foundation

The Anish Jadhav Memorial Foundation (Anish Jadhav Memorial Foundation) is a non-profit organization established in the loving memory of Anish Jadhav Sir. His vision, values and commitment to society continue to inspire us every day.

Anish Jadhav Memorial Foundation works towards education to create a positive and lasting impact on society.

We believe that every child deserves equal opportunities to learn, grow and succeed. Through scholarships, mentorship programs, skill development workshops and community initiatives, we strive to build a brighter future for the next generation.

Our foundation also collaborates with educational institutions, volunteers and partner organizations to create sustainable social impact across different communities.

OUR LEARNING METHODOLOGY

What We Do

Peer-to-Peer Learning

Students learn together by collaborating, discussing concepts, and teaching one another, fostering deep understanding and teamwork.

Mentor-Mentee Learning

Experienced mentors provide one-on-one guidance, industry insights, and personalized support to accelerate student growth.

Learning Through Challenges

Students learn by taking on meaningful challenges, solving practical problems, thinking critically, and building confidence through hands-on problem-solving.

Group Study

Students learn by studying together, discussing ideas, helping each other, sharing knowledge, and collaborating to build strong communication and teamwork skills.

Learning by Games

Students learn through fun and interactive games that help them understand concepts, practice skills, and enjoy the learning process.

Learning Space Activities

Students learn by taking part in simple hands-on activities where they can explore, practice, and understand concepts in a practical way.
