In proud collaboration with NavGurukul, we offer a completely fully-funded software engineering residential program designed for high-potential youth from under-served Indian communities.

Start Your Application

Who Can Apply?

Targeting passionate youth aged 16+ from lower-income backgrounds who carry a strong internal drive to learn, transform, and build a tech-enabled future.

Underprivileged Youths

Dedicated boys and girls from economically constrained families who have high aspiration levels but lack access to basic premium learning resources.

Daughters of Daily Earners

We actively empower daughters of auto drivers, watchmen, and helpers by providing a highly skilled, residential space for complete technical induction.

Graduates Lacking Direction

Young graduates seeking concrete, job-ready skills in software engineering but held back by high educational loan fees and job placement support.

The Admission Journey

Our four steps, highly transparent process, designed to identify high-potential candidates across all regions.

STEP 01

Profile Registration

Submit your basic academic information, online or directly reach out to our team of support for the next step.

STEP 02

Aptitude Evaluation

Aptitude, logical reasoning evaluation designed to check academic base, problem-solving and analytical mindset.

STEP 03

Scholarship Award

Successful candidates receive a 100% scholarship covering high-quality residential housing, food, books, and computers.

STEP 04

Intensive Prep & Job

Complete a structured education, domain, and skills curriculum, and then a close internship to help you land your first job.
