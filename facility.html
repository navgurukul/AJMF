Explore Our Campus Facilities

From modern classrooms to safe hostels, our campus is designed to support holistic growth. Here's a look at the facilities that make learning, living, and thriving a better experience for our students.

Spacious Classrooms

Our well-equipped classrooms provide a comfortable and inspiring learning environment with modern teaching aids and digital tools.

Computer Laboratories

Well-equipped computer labs with high-speed internet and the latest technology help students build digital and technical skills.

Library & Reading Room

A rich collection of books, digital resources, and a quiet reading space to foster curiosity, research, and independent learning.

Science Laboratories

Advanced science labs with modern equipment give students hands-on learning and real-world problem-solving skills.

Green & Safe Environment

A clean, green and secure campus with open spaces, fresh air, and proper safety measures for a healthy and happy learning life.

Sports & Recreation

Sports facilities and recreational areas help students stay active, build teamwork, and develop confidence beyond academics.

More
Than Just
Education

Student Accommodation

Safe and comfortable residential dormitories capable of housing 150 students, along with dedicated accommodation for instructors.

Kitchen & Dining

Hygienic, fully functional industrial-grade kitchen and spacious dining hall serving nutritious, balanced meals three times daily.

Eco-Friendly Infra

Committed to sustainability with Sewage Treatment Plant (STP), Solar Power generation, and Rainwater Harvesting.

CCTV Surveillance

Comprehensive 24/7 security monitoring with 64 HD CCTV cameras covering all indoor and outdoor campus areas.

24/7 Security Guards

Round-the-clock professional security staff stationed at all gates and entry points for student safety.

Water Softener Plant

Advanced water softening treatment facility ensuring soft, clean, and gentle water supply for campus utilities.

Sewage Treatment Plant

Zero-discharge eco-friendly sewage treatment plant recycling wastewater for campus greenery and gardening.

RO Water Plant

High-capacity commercial Reverse Osmosis water purification unit for 100% safe drinking water across campus.
