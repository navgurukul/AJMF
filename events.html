Moments That Make a Difference

Celebrating learning, leadership and community through meaningful events and activities.

AUG 15, 2024

Independence Day Celebration

Students came together to celebrate Independence Day with pride, unity and enthusiasm. The event included a flag hoisting ceremony, cultural performances and inspiring talks about freedom and responsibility.

Event Highlights

  • Flag hoisting ceremony
  • Cultural performances
  • Freedom & responsibility talk
  • Student participation
SEP 10, 2024

Mumbai Session

The Mumbai Session brought together students for an interactive learning experience, where they explored new ideas, shared perspectives and gained valuable insights from mentors and peers.

Event Highlights

  • Guest speaker session
  • Group discussions
  • Networking opportunity
  • Real-world insights
OCT 05, 2024

IT Hackathon

The IT Hackathon challenged students to think creatively, solve real-world problems and build innovative solutions using technology, teamwork and leadership.

Event Highlights

  • Team collaboration
  • Problem solving
  • Innovation & creativity
  • Presentation & evaluation
FEB 2026

Sports Activities

Students enthusiastically participated in various sports activities, including Kho-Kho, Volleyball and team games, promoting physical fitness, leadership and healthy competition.

Event Highlights

  • Kho-Kho & Volleyball Matches
  • Team Building Activities
  • Fitness & Outdoor Games
  • Sportsmanship & Competition
MAR 2026

Digital Literacy Workshop

Students attended interactive learning sessions conducted by industry experts, gaining hands-on experience with modern technologies, industry tools, and career guidance.

Event Highlights

  • Hands-on Industry Training
  • Tech Mentorship
  • Career Guidance
  • Practical Learning
JUN 30, 2026

Reward Ceremony 2026

The Reward Ceremony was organized to recognize and celebrate outstanding achievements of students, presenting awards and certificates for hard work, leadership, and excellence.

Event Highlights

  • Award & Certificate Distribution
  • Student Achievement Recognition
  • Inspirational Speeches
  • Celebration of Excellence
