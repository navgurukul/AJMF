📅 15 August 2026

Independence Day Celebration

Students gathered together to celebrate Independence Day with flag hoisting, patriotic songs, inspiring speeches, cultural performances and group activities. The event strengthened the spirit of unity, respect and love for our nation while encouraging students to participate with enthusiasm.

  • Flag Hoisting Ceremony

Women's Day Celebration

Women's Day was celebrated to recognize the achievements, strength and contributions of women. Students enjoyed motivational talks, cultural performances, games and appreciation activities that inspired confidence, equality and leadership.

EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • Motivational Guest Talk
  • Cultural Performances
  • Fun Games & Activities

IT Hackathon

Students worked in teams to solve real-world problems by building creative software projects within a limited time. The hackathon encouraged innovation, teamwork, problem-solving skills and practical learning through hands-on experience.

EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • Team-Based Coding Challenge
  • Real-World Problem Solving

  • Innovation & Collaboration

Sports Activities

Students enthusiastically participated in various sports activities, including Kho-Kho, Volleyball and other team games. These events promoted teamwork, discipline, physical fitness and healthy competition while creating unforgettable memories on campus.

EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • Kho-Kho & Volleyball Matches
  • Team Building Activities
  • Fitness & Outdoor Games
  • Sportsmanship & Healthy Competition

Students visited Mumbai to attend an interactive learning session conducted by industry experts. The experience provided valuable exposure to real-world work environments, modern technologies and career opportunities while encouraging practical learning beyond the classroom.
